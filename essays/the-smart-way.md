## Why Smart Questions are Important
In software engineering, how you ask for help matters just as much as how you actually write code.
When you reach a problem in your code, such as a bug or an error, feel free to ask experts or those more proficient than you.

To ask a question the "smart way" means to calm down, slow your pace, clean up your code and figure out what's broken or what's wrong.
Often times, you're able to find the answer on your own. However, asking a lazy question shows you didn't try.

## The "Smart Way"
A user, or poster, [noticed that other users on Stack Overflow were constantly posting massive, messy and broken code when asking for help regarding Python's Pandas library](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/20109391/how-to-make-good-reproducible-pandas-examples).
The poster posted a question asking the community to define a standard and clean template for creating small, easily replicable example dataframes.

Why was this smart? The poster explained why current methods such as, linking giant external files, were wasting everyone's time and frustrating.
Instead of focusing on one specific bug, the goal was to create a useful guide that could help the community ask better questions.
The poster kept the question short and focused only on important information required to create a clear and concise example.
Since the question was, other users and developers have turned it into a community wiki that is still used to teach users how to find and show problems clearly.

## The "Not Smart Way"
Another poster had asked a question on Stack Overflow, [wondering why their Python code was not working](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/25761920/why-doesnt-my-code-work-please-look-and-see-if-you-can-help-me).
The poster included their code and simply asked others to “look and see” what was wrong.

Why was this "not smart"? The poster did not clearly identify their specific problem, expected result, or actual result. They failed to demonstrate any debugging or research that they had already completed themselves before asking.
This made it difficult for users to give a quick and useful answer. So, users had to look through the code themselves and find problems, such as the loop not running and the `years` variable being changed.
This is why it's better to explain the problem clearly and at least try to find the solution before asking for help.

## Conclusion
In conclusion, asking questions the smart way is important because it helps others understand the problem and provide better answers.
A good question shows that the developer has taken their time to understand and and debug the issue before asking for help.
On the other hand, however, a vague question can waste time and be frustrating because others have to figure out the problem themselves.
By simply slowing down, explaining the problem clearly and showing what has been tried already, you can get more useful and valuable help and also become better at solving problems on your own.
